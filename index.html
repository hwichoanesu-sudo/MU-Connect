TRUST & SAFETY

Plan for problems before they grow.

Fake reviewsAllow one review per eligible order; use moderation and flag unusual patterns.
Fake sellersVerify seller contact and campus/business details before awarding a verified badge.
Payment disputesOnly mark paid after payment-provider server confirmation; record refunds and reversals.
Point abuseUse a points ledger and admin audit trail; never edit balances silently.
Low adoptionStart with a small pilot, interview users and measure actual repeat usage.
University differencesKeep each campus's rules, categories, moderators and business records separate.
